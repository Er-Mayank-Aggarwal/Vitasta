Artisanal Heritage of Jodhpur

Master Artistry on the Traditional Adda

Vitasta by Smita Saraswat is rooted in the regal majesty of Rajasthan. Every saree is crafted using handpicked pure fabrics and patient handwork including Aari, Gota Patti, Zardozi, Cutdana, Pitta, Meena and zero sequins.

On traditional wooden wooden frames called addas, skilled artisans spend weeks and sometimes months bringing a single saree to life, ensuring each drape reflects unparalleled precision and dignity.

👑 Regal Inspiration
Reimagined from royal women and queens of historic Rajasthan.
🪡 Patient Handwork
Intricate adda embroidery taking 15 to 30 days per masterpiece.
✨ Pure Fabrics
Chiffon, Habutai silk, Khaddi georgette & Banarasi weaves.
📹 Video Transparency
Complete pre-dispatch video shared for every individual order.

“From the choice of fabric to the final finishing, each piece reflects precision, patience and craftsmanship. At Vitasta, we aim to preserve the beauty of traditional artistry while creating sarees that feel refined, elegant and timeless.”
